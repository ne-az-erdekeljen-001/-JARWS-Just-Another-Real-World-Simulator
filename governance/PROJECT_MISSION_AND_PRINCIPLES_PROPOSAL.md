# JARWS — Project Mission and Principles

**Status: PROPOSED.** This document condenses the project's conceptual direction. It is not an accepted canonical specification, a product roadmap, or a claim that the described capabilities are implemented.

## Mission

JARWS aims to provide an open, modular environment where people and AI systems can collaboratively create, represent, model, simulate, analyze, test, and improve real or imagined systems.

People define the purpose and retain final authority over critical decisions. AI systems may contribute analysis and implementation within explicit, bounded responsibilities.

## What JARWS is

JARWS is intended as a general-purpose digital creation and development environment. It may include capabilities associated with games, simulators, or game engines, but its purpose is broader: supporting many kinds of worlds, models, systems, and collaborative work.

## Principles

1. **Human authority for critical decisions.** AI may work autonomously within an assigned scope, but it does not set its own foundational rules, permissions, or safety boundaries. Changes affecting governance, security, permissions, protected areas, or live systems require the defined human decision process.
2. **AI as a collaborator.** AI systems are cooperative components that can help plan, analyze, build, test, document, and coordinate work. Their responsibilities and access should be explicit.
3. **Modular responsibilities.** JARWS should be built from components with clear responsibilities, interfaces, and access boundaries. Governance, validation, approval, execution, and recovery should have distinct roles.
4. **Traceable decisions and changes.** Important decisions, sources, contributors, approvals, and changes should be recorded so people can understand what was decided, by whom, on what evidence, and with what scope.
5. **Responsible experimentation.** JARWS should support trying ideas and testing models while respecting human autonomy, reducing foreseeable harm, and making the limits of prototypes clear.
6. **Open and accessible creation.** The project aims to support knowledge sharing, modular extension, and community contribution. Over time, its creation environment should be usable by people without programming expertise as well as by developers.

## Scope and maturity

These principles describe direction, not implementation status. Proposed modules, real-world data connections, immersive devices, governance details, funding arrangements, and access policies require separate design and human decisions. No statement here makes a feature complete, secure, canonical, or approved for production.

The project's current prototype status and verification limits are described in the repository README and the relevant implementation proposals. Any conflict between this proposal and an accepted JARWS decision or specification must be resolved through the project's decision process.
